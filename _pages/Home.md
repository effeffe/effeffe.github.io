I am a Nuclear Physics PhD student in the [Nuclear Physics Group](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/research/nuclear/index.aspx) at the **University of Birmingham**, supervised by Dr Jack Bishop, Prof Carl Wheldon and Prof Tzany Kokalova Wheldon.

My research sits between computation and experiment. The core of my thesis is a Geant4 Monte Carlo simulation of $\alpha$-induced $^{9}$Be neutron sources (AmBe and PuBe) built around an analytical $^{9}$Be($\alpha$,n)$^{12}$C primary generator, together with the moderation and detector response that follow. Alongside this I work on detector-response modelling for LaBr$_3$ arrays and double-sided silicon-strip telescopes, a ROOT/C++ analysis framework to replace legacy tools, the digital acquisition system for the MC40 cyclotron, and analogue front-end electronics for portable detectors.

I have taken part in radioactive-beam campaigns at **CERN ISOLDE** (ISS, $^{7}$Be(d,p)$^{8}$Be) and **TRIUMF** ISAC-II ($^{9}$Li on boron), and I contribute to open-source scientific software — my rewrite of the Geant4-MPI interface was merged into Geant4 11.4.

See my [publications](/publications/), [projects](/projects/) and [CV](/cv/), or get in touch by [email](mailto:filippo.falezza@outlook.com).
