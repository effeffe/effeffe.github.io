## Supervision

- **Y3 Group Study** — _Development of a Machine Learning Algorithm for Unknown Isotopes Identification_ (2023–2026).
  Students acquire experimental spectra, generate Geant4 training data, design a portable scintillator detector
  with preamplifier and shaping-amplifier electronics, and train an isotope-identification model.

## Laboratory demonstrating

- **Y3 Nuclear Laboratory** (2022–2026) — radiation-detection techniques across $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ and
  X-ray sources, spectroscopy, and detector timing with a time-to-amplitude converter (TAC).
- **Y1 Physics Laboratory** (2022) — introductory experimental methods and error analysis.
