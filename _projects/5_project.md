A student group-study project I supervise (2023–2026), aiming at automatic identification of
unknown gamma-emitting isotopes from a low-cost portable detector.

- Portable scintillator detector with a charge-sensitive preamplifier and shaping amplifier
  designed in-house.
- Geant4 used to generate large, labelled training spectra covering detector response and
  geometry effects.
- A classifier trained on the simulated data and validated against measured sources.
